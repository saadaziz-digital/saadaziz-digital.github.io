GoHighLevel Funnel Automations

Three GHL workflows built for a home-services client to take a lead from first contact through to a booked, confirmed appointment — with no manual step in between. Client details anonymized.

Lead intake & routing

Triggered the moment a Facebook Lead Form is submitted.

Facebook lead form submitted→ Assigned to rep→ Opportunity created→ Lead tagged→ Internal notification→ Autoresponder email + SMS→ Meta Conversion API fired

Appointment confirmation & reminders

Branches on appointment status, then reminds automatically before the visit.

Appointment status changes→ Branch: proposed / in-house / follow-up→ SMS + email confirmation→ Reminder at 24 hrs→ Reminder at 7 hrs→ Sales rep texted directly

SMS keyword & opt-in handling

Parses inbound SMS replies for keywords and responds automatically, no rep needed.

Customer replies via SMS→ Keyword detected (start / join / help / info)→ Matching auto-response sent→ Opt-in status & tags updated
Turns a raw Meta lead into a confirmed, reminded appointment without a rep touching it manually — while still routing every keyword reply and status change to the right person in real time.
