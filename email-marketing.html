Types of Campaigns I Run

Not every email needs the same depth of personalization — the right level depends on list size, data available, and what the campaign is trying to do.

Targeted

Segment-based

Lists segmented by firmographic criteria — industry, company size, role — so the message fits a defined audience instead of going out as a broad blast.

Personalized

Recipient-specific

Emails built around the specific recipient — name, company, role, a relevant pain point — pulled from enrichment data, not just a first-name mail-merge.

Hyper-Personalized

Trigger-based

Built around something specific to that one prospect — a recent company event, a tool they use, a competitor mention — the detail that makes an email read as researched, not templated.
